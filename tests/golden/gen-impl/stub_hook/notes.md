`--stub-policy hook`: a stub calls `mt_not_implemented("<signature>")`, declared by a header of its own (`mt_not_implemented.h`, written next to the others and included by the source), and then returns what `default` would: nothing for `void`, `{}` for a value. A type that `{}` cannot build (`std::runtime_error`) gets the abort after the hook, with a warning that says so. The default handler throws; `mt_set_not_implemented_handler` replaces it. No `mt-stub-may-pass`: the hook is what fails the test.
