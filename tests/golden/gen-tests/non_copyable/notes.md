A class that deletes its copy or its move says so in a case of `static_assert`s, before the cases of its members: `IsNotCopyable` for the copy constructor and the copy assignment it deletes (one assertion each: `!std::is_copy_constructible<Handle>::value`), `IsNotMovable` for the moves. Like `Inherits` they are the whole test, so there is no placeholder and they pass. Private or public, the deletion counts. A defaulted copy (`Plain`) is not asserted, and a class with nothing else to test (`Lock`) is planned for the case alone.
